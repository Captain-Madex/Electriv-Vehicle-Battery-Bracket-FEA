PROJECT OBJECTIVES
=================================================================
Static structural analysis of an end plate + L-bracket (with gusset)
+ clamp plate supporting a battery block (EV battery bracket FEM)
=================================================================


1. MAIN OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Determine the structural response of the end plate + L-bracket/gusset
+ clamp plate assembly under the 294.3 N per-bracket load (F = mg/2,
m = 60 kg block shared by two brackets, g = 9.81 m/s^2), and check
whether the design is safe against yield.


2. STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS OBJECTIVES
---------------------------------
Using a 2D linear-elastic static model (plane thickness t = 80 mm,
bonded parts, fixed end-plate left face and flange bottom):

  1. Total deformation
     - Find the maximum total deformation and where it occurs.
  2. Equivalent (von Mises) stress
     - Find the maximum value and its location.
  3. Principal stresses
     - Find the maximum and minimum principal stress.
  4. Stress components
     - Obtain normal stress X, normal stress Y and shear stress XY.
  5. Directional deformation
     - Obtain the X and Y displacement fields.
  6. Equivalent elastic strain
     - Obtain the field and its maximum.
  7. Support reactions
     - Find the force and moment at the two fixed edges.
     - Check equilibrium: the vertical reaction should balance 294.3 N.
  8. Stress concentration
     - Assess the web / gusset / flange junction.
  9. Factor of safety
     - Calculate the factor of safety against yield (the material
       yield strength must be confirmed first).


3. COMPUTATIONAL / SOLVER OBJECTIVES
------------------------------------
  1. Develop an independent Python FEM solver (four-node Q4 plane-stress
     elements) that reads the ANSYS 18.2 CDB mesh and solves K u = F.
  2. Apply the consistent edge load (5.886 N/mm over the 50 mm loaded
     edge), the fixed supports and the bonded-interface constraints.
  3. Recover element strains and stresses and produce contour plots for
     all ten result quantities (total / X / Y deformation, von Mises,
     sigma_x, sigma_y, tau_xy, equivalent strain, max and min principal
     stress).
  4. Run a mesh-convergence study over the five meshes (2.00, 1.50,
     1.00, 0.50 and 0.25 mm) and judge whether the peak results become
     mesh-independent.
  5. Validate the Python solver against ANSYS results for every mesh,
     using signed difference, absolute error and relative error.


4. VALIDATION OBJECTIVES (OPEN ISSUES TO RESOLVE)
-------------------------------------------------
  1. Find the cause of the consistent factor of about 1.98 between the
     Q4 and ANSYS maximum total deformation. Do not correct it by simply
     dividing the Q4 result by two.
  2. Check that both models use identical load, supports, materials,
     section thickness, geometry and interface coordinates.
  3. Compare nodal Ux and Uy at identical physical coordinates, not only
     the global maxima.
  4. Compare stress at matched integration points and on the same side
     of each bonded interface, and explain the differences in sigma_x,
     tau_xy and maximum principal stress.
  5. Confirm the coordinate-system mapping of the ANSYS labels
     ("Normal Stress 2", "Directional Deformation 2") to global X and Y.
  6. Check that the excluded degenerate (collapsed) shell elements are
     not near critical regions.


5. MODEL ITEMS TO CONFIRM
-------------------------
  a) Contact length: 40 mm in the drawings vs the 50 mm clamp length
     loaded in the 2D model.
  b) Load arm and flange: d = 90 mm and 120 mm flange (M = 26.5 N.m) in
     the free body vs d = 80 mm and 110 mm flange (M = 23.5 N.m) in the
     2D model.
  c) End plate height: 340 mm in the side view vs 140 mm in the 2D
     model (the 2D model is a local section).
  d) Bracket width: whether one 80 mm bracket per end carries the full
     294.3 N, given the 300 mm block depth.
  e) Gusset slope start (about 45 mm below the web top) is estimated
     from photos, not measured.
  f) Flange-bottom fixed support: the load goes almost directly into
     the support, so the moment M is barely transferred to the end
     plate. Confirm this is the intended boundary condition.
  g) Material: not specified on the drawings. Confirm the properties and
     yield strength (steel E = 200 GPa, nu = 0.3; aluminium E = 70 GPa,
     nu = 0.33 for the bracket in the current CDB mapping).
  h) Thickness: confirm one consistent value (the solver summaries use
     80 mm; the literature-review file lists 2 mm).


6. ASSUMPTIONS
--------------
  - Linear elastic material, small deformations, static loading.
  - Self-weight of the plate, bracket and clamp is neglected.
  - Parts are bonded (no separation or sliding).
  - The load is a uniform pressure on the clamp plate top face.
  - The load is shared equally by the two ends (symmetry).
  - The Q4 solver is a membrane-only idealisation of SHELL181 (no
    rotations, bending or transverse shear), so results are estimates
    and not a full reproduction of ANSYS shell results.


7. DELIVERABLES
---------------
  - Numerical result files (summary, nodal displacements, element
    results) for each mesh.
  - Contour figures for all ten result quantities, plus mesh/support/
    load and deformed-shape figures.
  - ANSYS vs Q4 comparison table and explanation report.
  - Mesh-convergence discussion and a statement of validation status.


8. FUTURE OBJECTIVES
--------------------
  - Identify regions where material can be reduced (lightweight design
    or topology optimisation) while keeping the factor of safety.
  - Extend the analysis to modal / vibration behaviour.
  - Extend the 2D section to a 3D model that represents bracket width.
  - Prepare the results and validated workflow for a journal paper.
