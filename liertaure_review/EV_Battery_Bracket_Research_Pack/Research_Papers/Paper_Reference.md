# Research paper reference

## Primary EV battery-bracket literature source

**Title:** Optimization design of battery bracket for new energy vehicles based on 3D printing technology  
**Authors:** Guoqing Zhang, Xueyan Li, Junxin Li, Xiaoyu Zhou, and Yongsheng Zhou  
**Journal:** Scientific Reports, Volume 14, Article 13461 (2024)  
**Published:** 12 June 2024  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-64393-x  
**Open-access full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11166990/  
**Publisher page / PDF access:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-64393-x

### Why this paper is relevant
This paper directly studies an electric-vehicle battery bracket using finite-element strength assessment and topology optimization. It discusses bracket geometry, structural loading, displacement, von Mises stress, factor of safety, lightweighting, geometric reconstruction, and manufacturing/assembly verification.

### Results reported by the paper
- Optimized bracket maximum displacement: 3.20 mm.
- Maximum von Mises stress after optimization: 240.7 MPa.
- Minimum factor of safety: 1.
- Optimized mass: 0.348 kg.
- Reported mass reduction: approximately 49.2% (the paper contains slightly varying percentage statements in different passages).

### Important distinction
These are the paper's published results, not the results of our bracket model. They must not be presented as our ANSYS or Python FEM results.
