PROBLEM STATEMENT
=================================================================
Static structural analysis of an end plate + L-bracket (with gusset)
+ clamp plate supporting a battery block
=================================================================


1. SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
---------------------
A battery block of mass m = 60 kg (600 mm long x 300 mm deep x 250 mm
high) is mounted between two parallel vertical end plates. The clear
span between the end plates is 740 mm and the end plate height is
340 mm.

Each end plate carries an L-bracket with a triangular gusset on its
inner face. A clamp plate sits on the flange of each L-bracket. The
battery block rests on the two clamp plates, with 40 mm of contact
at each end. The block's weight is therefore shared equally between
the left and right brackets.

The system is symmetric about the mid-span cut plane, so only one half
of the block and one bracket assembly needs to be analysed (the other
half is its mirror image).


2. LOADING AND STATICS (HALF-BLOCK FREE BODY)
---------------------------------------------
  Gravity                  g = 9.81 m/s^2
  Total block weight       W = m g = 60 x 9.81 = 588.6 N
  Load per bracket         F = W/2 = mg/2 = 294.3 N (downward, -Y)

Free body of the half block (300 mm long):
  - Weight of half block, W/2 = 294.3 N, acts at its centre of gravity.
  - Cut plane at mid-span: symmetry, Ux = 0, shear = 0.
  - Block pushes on the clamp plate: B = 294.3 N.
  - Reaction at the end plate: R = 294.3 N (upward).
  - Moment at the end plate: M = 26.5 N.m, from F x d with d = 90 mm.


3. 2D MODEL GEOMETRY (all dimensions in mm)
-------------------------------------------
Model type: 2D static structural, plane thickness t = 80 mm (out of
the page, Z direction). X is horizontal (away from the end plate),
Y is vertical.

  End plate         15 wide x 140 tall.

  L-bracket web     8 thick, 100 tall, attached to the right face of
                    the end plate. It starts 20 below the top and ends
                    20 above the bottom of the 140 mm end plate section.

  Gusset            Vertical edge of the web runs approx. 45 mm down
                    from the web top, then slopes down to meet the
                    flange top at 45 mm from the end plate face.
                    (The 45 mm slope start is estimated from photos,
                    not measured.)

  Flange            8 thick, 110 long, measured from the end plate face.

  Clamp plate       50 long x 6 thick, on top of the flange. It starts
                    55 mm from the end plate face (45 + 10 gap) and
                    ends 5 mm before the flange tip. Its centre is
                    80 mm from the end plate face.

  Horizontal layout from end plate face: 45 (gusset foot) + 10 (gap)
  + 50 (clamp plate) + 5 (overhang) = 110.


4. BOUNDARY CONDITIONS
----------------------
  A  Fixed support on two edges:
       (i)  the left face of the end plate, and
       (ii) the bottom of the flange (110 mm long).

  B  Force F = mg/2 = 294.3 N, downward (-Y), applied as a uniform
     pressure on the top face of the clamp plate (50 mm x 80 mm):
         p = 294.3 N / (50 x 80 mm^2) = 0.0736 MPa
     The arm of F from the end plate face is 80 mm.

  Mass per bracket: 30 kg (60 kg block / 2 brackets).


5. MATERIAL
-----------
Not specified on the drawings. To be defined (for example, structural
steel with E = 200 GPa, nu = 0.3, yield strength to be confirmed).


6. OBJECTIVE
------------
Determine, for the end plate + L-bracket/gusset + clamp plate assembly
under the 294.3 N per-bracket load:
  - total deformation and its maximum location,
  - equivalent (von Mises) stress and maximum principal stress,
  - support reactions (force and moment) at the fixed edges,
  - stress concentration at the web / gusset / flange junction,
  - factor of safety against yield.


7. ASSUMPTIONS
--------------
  - Linear elastic material, small deformations, static loading.
  - Self-weight of the plate, bracket and clamp is neglected.
  - Parts are bonded (no separation or sliding between end plate,
    bracket and clamp plate).
  - The load is a uniform pressure on the clamp plate top face.
  - The load is shared equally by the two ends (symmetry).


8. NOTES / ITEMS TO CONFIRM
---------------------------
  a) Contact length: the 3D, side and free body views show 40 mm of
     block contact on the clamp, but the 2D model applies the load
     over the full 50 mm clamp plate length.

  b) Load arm and flange length: the free body view uses d = 90 mm and
     a 120 mm flange (M = 26.5 N.m). The 2D model uses d = 80 mm and a
     110 mm flange (M = 294.3 x 0.080 = 23.5 N.m). The two differ by
     10 mm.

  c) End plate height: 340 mm in the side view vs 140 mm in the 2D
     model. The 2D model appears to be a local section only.

  d) Bracket width: the block is 300 mm deep, but the model thickness
     is 80 mm. This assumes one 80 mm wide bracket per end carries the
     full 294.3 N. Confirm the number and width of brackets.

  e) Gusset slope start (about 45 mm below the web top) is estimated.

  f) With the flange bottom fixed, the clamp load goes almost directly
     into the support. The moment M is then barely transferred to the
     end plate. Confirm this is the intended boundary condition.
